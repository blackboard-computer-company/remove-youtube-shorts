Remove YouTube Shorts

This extension automatically hides Shorts and Playables from your YouTube homepage, sidebar, and feeds.

Your YouTube homepage opens Subscriptions instead of recommendations.

Blocked websites

Block a domain or a path, like youtube.com/shorts/*. Paths match prefixes; * matches any text. Subdomains are included.
